In Catania rain does not ruin the day: it moves it. The historic centre is compact, distances between stops are short and many of the most interesting places are indoors. You only need a sensible order, so you do not cross half the city in the downpour.

> **In short.** Keep the stops close, alternate an indoor place with a short outdoor stretch, and check opening hours: museums and monuments change days and time slots.

## First rule: stay in one area

The worst route in the rain is the one that jumps from one end of the city to the other. Pick one area and fill it: the stretch between Via Etnea, Piazza Duomo and the fish market is the most comfortable, because everything is a few minutes on foot.

:::widget rain

## 1. Teatro Massimo Bellini {coperto}

The theatre named after Vincenzo Bellini is one of the city's landmark places, right in the centre. If there is no performance on, check whether guided visits are offered: availability changes during the year.

## 2. Castello Ursino and the civic museum {coperto}

The Swabian-era castle houses the civic museum. It is a solid stop for an hour or two indoors and takes a few minutes to reach from the Duomo area.

## 3. Monastery of the Benedictines of San Nicolò l'Arena {coperto}

One of the great monastic complexes in Europe, now a university campus and part of the UNESCO listing of the late Baroque towns of the Val di Noto. Guided visits usually need a booking: if it rains, decide early in the morning.

## 4. The Cathedral of Sant'Agata {coperto}

The Duomo faces Piazza Duomo, with the Elephant Fountain and the Liotru, the symbol of the city. The interior is a perfect shelter for a break between two outdoor stretches.

## 5. The Achillean Baths under the Cathedral {coperto}

In the basements of the Duomo area there are Roman-era rooms. It is the classic stop you do not know you want until it rains: dry, unusual and very close to the centre.

## 6. Lunch at the fish market, but under cover {aperto}

The fish market is the popular heart of Catania, mostly outdoors and in the morning. When it rains, the answer is to arrive early and sit down in one of the covered places nearby, instead of walking between the stalls.

## 7. A proper coffee break {coperto}

In Catania an hour's pause between two stops is not wasted time: it is the best way to let the storm pass. Pick a café close to your next stop, not to where you started.

## How to order the day

:::tappe
- Early morning | One indoor stop | Book if needed: the Benedictine Monastery or Castello Ursino.
- Noon | Lunch nearby | Stay where you are, with no moves in the rain.
- Afternoon | Second stop in the same area | The Duomo and the Achillean Baths, then a short outdoor stretch when it stops.
- Evening | Short walk | If the sky opens, a stroll through the centre towards Via Etnea.
:::

:::consiglio With wet lava the paving is slippery: rubber-soled shoes, and keep the longest stop for when you are already dry.
