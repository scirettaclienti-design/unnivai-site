I build digital platforms and products with artificial intelligence. UNNIVAI starts from a simple idea: travel works when the route is made of real places and adapts to your day, not when it is a generic list.

On this blog I write itineraries and guides for people travelling in Italy, starting from Sicily. Every article shows the date of its last check and points to official sources for opening hours and bookings, which change often.
