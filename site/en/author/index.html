Why this blog

UNNIVAI starts from a simple idea: travel works when the route is made of real places and adapts to your day, not when it is a generic list. Here I write itineraries and guides for people travelling in Italy. I am not a licensed tour guide: the guides rely on official, verifiable sources, with the date of the last check, and the method we use to write them is public, including the use of artificial intelligence.
