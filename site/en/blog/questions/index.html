Questions people really ask us.

The most frequent questions about travelling in Italy, itineraries, rain and planning with AI, with short answers and links to the guides.

What is Catania's typical dish?

Pasta alla Norma: pasta with tomato sauce, fried aubergine, salted ricotta and basil. The name recalls the opera by Bellini, who was from Catania.

In Catania, is it arancino or arancina?

In Catania it is arancino, masculine, and traditionally cone-shaped, like Etna. In other parts of Sicily it is called arancina.

Read: What to eat in Catania →
How much does it cost to eat in Catania?

Street tastings cost a few euros: an arancino around €2, granita with brioche around €4, a pasta alla Norma about €8 in simple places. Figures are indicative averages and vary from place to place.

When is it best to eat around the fish market?

In the morning, when the market is open: the street-food stands around it come alive around mid-morning.

What are the opening hours of the Catania fish market?

The sources we consulted indicate opening Monday to Saturday and closing on Sunday, with slightly different hours (from about 7 or 8 until 2 or 2:30 pm). The practical rule is to go by mid-morning: after noon many stalls start to pack up.

Is the Catania fish market open on Sunday?

No, according to the sources consulted it is closed on Sunday.

How long does a visit take?

30 to 60 minutes to walk between the stalls; about two hours if you stop to eat nearby.

Is there an entrance fee?

Does it rain a lot in Catania?

Rain falls mostly between autumn and winter and often arrives in downpours. That is why it is worth having an indoor plan B for half a day instead of cancelling everything.

Can you explore Catania's historic centre in the rain?

Yes, but wear the right shoes: the lava paving gets slippery. Alternate short outdoor stretches with indoor visits that are close to each other.
