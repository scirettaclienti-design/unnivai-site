Privacy

Data processing notice

In short: we ask for your email and, if you wish, your city. They are used only to write to you when access is ready and to understand where to launch. We do not sell your data.

Who is the controller

Ivano Sciretta, owner of the UNNIVAI project. Contact for any data request: sciretta.clienti@gmail.com.

What data we collect

  • Email (required to sign up).
  • City or area of interest (optional) and the corresponding region.
  • Technical sign-up data: date and time, and the link or channel you come from, if any.

We do not ask for payment data and we do not detect your GPS position. The cities chosen are also counted anonymously, with no link to your email.

Why and on what basis

To send you the early-access invitation and communications about the launch of UNNIVAI, and to produce aggregate statistics on the cities requested. The legal basis is your consent (art. 6.1.a GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time without consequences.

For how long

Until you withdraw consent and in any case no longer than 24 months from sign-up, unless you consent again.

Who sees the data

The data is stored on Supabase, in a database in the European Union (Ireland). The site is hosted by Vercel, which may process technical browsing data also outside the EU with appropriate contractual safeguards. We do not give data to third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies and external services

The site does not use profiling cookies or analytics tools that identify you. Fonts and 3D graphics are served by the site itself, with no external tracking services. The hosting provider records technical browsing data (such as the IP address) to ensure security and operation.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability of your data (arts. 15–22 GDPR) by writing to the address above: we reply within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

Minors

The service is not intended for anyone under 14.
